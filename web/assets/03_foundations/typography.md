# AVGL Typography

- Primary UI/brand sans: **Inter** or **Helvetica Neue**; fall back to Arial/system sans.
- Editorial/brand statement serif: **Georgia** or an equivalent restrained editorial serif.
- Technical metadata/IR: **IBM Plex Mono** or system monospace.

No font files are included in this bundle.

## Suggested scale
- Display: 64/68, -2% tracking
- H1: 44/50
- H2: 32/38
- H3: 22/28
- Body: 16/24
- Small: 13/19
- Metadata: 11/16, +8% tracking
